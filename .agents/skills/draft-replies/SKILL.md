# Draft replies

## Procedure

1. Resolve the recipient and related task/topic.
2. Retrieve the recipient's role, language, recent interactions, commitments, and related work.
3. Retrieve current task claims, evidence, decisions, risks, and unresolved questions.
4. Identify the communication purpose: inform, ask, unblock, clarify, escalate, or commit.
5. Do not invent deadlines, ownership, agreement, or completed work.
6. Draft a concise usable version in the recipient's language.
7. Add a diplomatic or email version only when useful.
8. State to the human operator what context supports the draft and what remains uncertain.
9. Persist the draft in the outbox only when requested or when the active workflow requires it.
10. Never send or publish through an external connector without explicit approval.
