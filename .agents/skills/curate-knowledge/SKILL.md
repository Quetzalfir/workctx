# Curate knowledge

## Procedure

1. Retrieve existing canonical entities, aliases, and typed relations.
2. Decide whether to update, merge, split, supersede, or create.
3. Prefer improving an existing entity over producing redundant notes.
4. Preserve stable IDs, aliases, source observations, and history.
5. Separate current facts, historical facts, inference, and assumptions.
6. Do not infer functional ownership merely because a system displays or orchestrates a domain.
7. Use typed relationships and exact evidence references.
8. Stage changes in one transaction proposal.
9. Validate references and context-pack behavior after apply.
10. Regenerate indexes and views; do not edit generated backlinks manually.

## Promotion rule

Keep investigation-specific findings near their task until they are useful beyond that task. Promote only reusable, stable knowledge into shared entities.
